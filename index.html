(02) — How We Work

FOUR STAGES

Every engagement moves through the same four stages, whether it's a two-week security audit or a year-long platform build.

01
ASSESS
We audit your current stack, infrastructure and risk surface before recommending anything — a short technical review that sets scope and priority.
02
ARCHITECT
We design the system: cloud topology, data flow, and security boundaries, reviewed with your team before a line of code is written.
03
BUILD
Development runs in short sprints with staging environments from day one, so what you review is close to what ships to production.
04
SUPPORT
Post-launch monitoring, incident response and a documented runbook — your team can operate it independently or keep us on retainer.
