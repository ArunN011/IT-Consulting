COMMON QUESTIONS

Technology Advisory FAQ

When should we use technology advisory?

Advisory is useful when you are evaluating a major technology decision, planning modernization, reviewing architecture, or defining a technical roadmap.

Can advisory support an existing project?

Yes. Advisory can be used alongside an active project to review architecture, technical direction, risks, and delivery choices.

Do we need to prepare technical documents?

Existing architecture diagrams, documentation, requirements and infrastructure information can help, but the engagement can also begin with a discovery discussion.

Can the engagement be short term?

The advisory model can support focused decision-making as well as broader technology planning engagements.
